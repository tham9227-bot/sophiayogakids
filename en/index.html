Hello, I'm Sophia! 🌿

Planting seeds of yoga in young hearts

I graduated in English Language from Thuongmai University and spent more than six years teaching English to children, with a 120-hour TEFL certificate. The children taught me something simple: they learn best when they move, play, laugh and are free to be themselves.

Yoga came into my life in 2017 with a 200-hour teacher training at Golden Hearts Academy, followed by a 300-hour Advanced training and kids yoga teacher training in 2022, and the 500-hour Master of Yoga & Meditation, all Yoga Alliance standard.

Where English and yoga meet, my bilingual yoga program was born: children become a tree, a star or a little snake, breathing deeply while learning English through stories, songs and games.

“In Sophia's class there is no right or wrong. Only laughter, adventure stories and deep, happy breaths.”

Kids Yoga Teacher Training 30 hours

Sophia's core course: a story-based method to teach yoga through games and breathing, so you can confidently start your own kids yoga class.

📖

What you learn

How to build a 30–45 minute yoga class for children aged 3 to 6, storytelling through poses, games and songs, and breathing exercises that help children calm down and focus.

🤝

How you learn

Hands-on practice with personal corrections from Sophia, in person at Ecopark or online via Zoom. A certificate is awarded at the end of the course.
